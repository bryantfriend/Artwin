# Interior material sources

The bundled maps are 1K JPEG downloads of these Poly Haven CC0 assets (downloaded 15 September 2026):

- `wood-color.jpg`, `wood-normal.jpg`: [Wood Table 001](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table_001), diffuse and OpenGL normal maps.
- `fabric-normal.jpg`, `fabric-roughness.jpg`: [Fabric Pattern 07](https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_pattern_07), OpenGL normal and roughness maps, by Rob Tuytel.

[Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license): CC0, including commercial use and redistribution. Project renders and Poly Haven branding are not included.

The renderer neutralizes the wood stain and tints the grain to the project's oak/walnut palette. Only the fabric surface maps are used, retaining project-specific upholstery colors. All files are served locally beneath the site's base path. They describe illustrative interior finishes, not ARTWIN's contracted material specifications.
