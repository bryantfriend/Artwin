## Implemented synthesis

- Six residence cards with a 2D drawing beside a screenshot from that same apartment's existing 3D viewer. On mobile, the two images stay together above the details. An enlarged dialog preserves both views.
- Bedroom/area filters, area sorting, result counts and recoverable empty states.
- Favorite plans retained on this device, with a saved-only filter.
- Comparison of two or three selected plans, including images and area/bedroom/bathroom metrics. This is our decision-support feature; the table above does not claim every reviewed builder offers comparison.
- Rounded cards, quieter borders, prominent imagery, concise features and Artwin-yellow actions.
- Consultation entry points, a project selector and the published Artwin consultant roster.

The preview images are captures of this application's procedural models, not Artwin's photorealistic reference renders. The existing approximation note remains visible. No unverified pricing, inventory or construction-status data was added.
